“Step Into 1980” was the YouTube title, and for once it underpromises. This dual-level brick home in Jamboree Heights hasn't just kept its retro details — by the agent's own account, it hasn't been meaningfully touched in forty years, and every room is here to prove it.

Upstairs runs the showpiece: a fully intact mustard-yellow kitchen with timber trim and the original patterned tile underfoot, opening to a living and dining area finished in striped wallpaper under a sunburst mirror, with a balcony framed by brick archways for whoever wants the view without leaving the building.

Downstairs adds a second act rather than repeating the first: another living area built around a wet bar and an orange-tiled kitchenette, a fifth bedroom doing duty as a study in full-pile gold shag carpet, and an ensuited bedroom that means the ground floor could run almost independently of the one above it.

The bathrooms alone are worth the inspection: one ensuite tiled floor-to-ceiling in olive and mustard, the main bathroom answering in green, and not a single fitting that looks like it's been swapped since the original build. This one's already sold, but the listing's still up if you want the full tour.
